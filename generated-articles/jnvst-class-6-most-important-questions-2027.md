### Mental Ability Questions (30+ MCQs with Answers)

The Mental Ability section carries the highest weightage in JNVST. Practising these **JNVST Class 6 practice questions** for mental ability is crucial.

#### Mirror Image Questions

**Q1.** If a clock shows 3:15, what will be the time in its mirror image?

- A) 8:45 ✓
- B) 9:15
- C) 8:15
- D) 9:45

**Answer:** A) 8:45
**Explanation:** In mirror image, the hour hand at 3 becomes at 9, and the minute hand at 3 (15 minutes) becomes at 9 (45 minutes). So mirror time = 12:00 - 3:15 = 8:45.

**Q2.** Choose the mirror image of the word "MATHS":

- A) SHTAM
- B) SHTAᗺ
- C) SHTAᗡ
- D) SHTAᗺ

**Answer:** B) SHTAᗺ
**Explanation:** In mirror image, letters reverse and individual letters flip horizontally.

#### Figure Series Questions

**Q3.** Complete the series: ○, △, □, ○, △, □, ○, ?

- A) ○
- B) △ ✓
- C) □
- D) ◇

**Answer:** B) △
**Explanation:** The pattern repeats every 3 shapes: ○, △, □. After ○ comes △.

**Q4.** Find the next figure in the series: ▲, ▲▲, ▲▲▲, ?

- A) ▲▲▲▲ ✓
- B) ▲▲
- C) ▲▲▲▲▲
- D) ▲

**Answer:** A) ▲▲▲▲
**Explanation:** Each step adds one triangle. Next will have 4 triangles.

#### Coding-Decoding Questions

**Q5.** If APPLE is coded as ELPPA, how is MANGO coded?

- A) OGNAM ✓
- B) OGNMA
- C) OGAMN
- D) ONGAM

**Answer:** A) OGNAM
**Explanation:** The word is reversed. APPLE → ELPPA, MANGO → OGNAM.

**Q6.** If A = 1, B = 2, C = 3, ..., what is the code for "DOG"?

- A) 26
- B) 27
- C) 24
- D) 25

**Answer:** A) 26
**Explanation:** D = 4, O = 15, G = 7. Total = 4 + 15 + 7 = 26.

**Q7.** In a certain code, "MONKEY" is written as "NPOOLZ". How is "TIGER" written in that code?

- A) UJHFS ✓
- B) UJHGS
- C) UIHFS
- D) VJHFS

**Answer:** A) UJHFS
**Explanation:** Each letter is shifted +1: M→N, O→P, N→O, K→L, E→F, Y→Z. Similarly, T→U, I→J, G→H, E→F, R→S.

#### Analogy Questions

**Q8.** Doctor : Hospital :: Teacher : ?

- A) School ✓
- B) Office
- C) Market
- D) Garden

**Answer:** A) School
**Explanation:** A doctor works in a hospital. A teacher works in a school.

**Q9.** Eye : See :: Ear : ?

- A) Sound
- B) Hear ✓
- C) Nose
- D) Mouth

**Answer:** B) Hear
**Explanation:** Eye is used to see. Ear is used to hear.

**Q10.** 2 : 8 :: 3 : ?

- A) 12
- B) 27 ✓
- C) 9
- D) 18

**Answer:** B) 27
**Explanation:** 2³ = 8, 3³ = 27. The relationship is cube.

#### Number Series Questions

**Q11.** Find the next number: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?

- A) 40
- B) 42 ✓
- C) 36
- D) 44

**Answer:** B) 42
**Explanation:** Differences: 4, 6, 8, 10, 12. Next number = 30 + 12 = 42.

**Q12.** Complete the series: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, ?

- A) 11
- B) 13 ✓
- C) 10
- D) 12

**Answer:** B) 13
**Explanation:** Fibonacci series. Each number is the sum of the two preceding numbers: 5 + 8 = 13.

**Q13.** Find the missing number: 3, 9, 27, 81, ?

- A) 162
- B) 243 ✓
- C) 216
- D) 324

**Answer:** B) 243
**Explanation:** Each number is multiplied by 3. 81 × 3 = 243.

- B) 14 ✓

- A) All roses are red
- B) Some roses may be red ✓
- C) No roses are red
- D) All flowers are roses

**Answer:** B) Some roses may be red
**Explanation:** Since some flowers are red and all roses are flowers, some roses may or may not be red.

- A) 25
- B) 26 ✓

**Q26.** How many lines of symmetry does a square have?

- A) 2
- B) 4 ✓
- C) 6
- D) 8

**Answer:** B) 4
**Explanation:** A square has 4 lines of symmetry: 2 diagonals and 2 through the midpoints of opposite sides.

- A) 10 cm ✓
- B) 12 cm
- C) 14 cm
- D) 48 cm

**Answer:** A) 10 cm
**Explanation:** Using Pythagorean theorem: √(8² + 6²) = √(64 + 36) = √100 = 10 cm.

**Q28.** How many triangles are in a star inside a triangle figure?

**Q29.** Count the number of squares in a 3×3 grid:

- B) Full circle

**Q32.** What is the smallest 5-digit number using the digits 0, 1, 2, 3, 4 (each used once)?

- A) 10,234 ✓
- B) 12,340
- C) 10,243

**Explanation:** For smallest number, arrange digits in ascending order with non-zero digit at first place: 10,234.

**Q34.** Simplify: 3/4 + 2/3

- A) 17/12 ✓
- B) 5/7
- C) 6/12
- D) 5/12

**Answer:** A) 17/12
**Explanation:** LCM of 4 and 3 = 12. 3/4 = 9/12, 2/3 = 8/12. 9/12 + 8/12 = 17/12.

**Q35.** Convert 0.75 to a fraction in simplest form.

- A) 75/100

**Q36.** Which is greater: 2/3 or 3/5?

- C) Both are equal
- D) Cannot be determined

**Q37.** What is 25% of 200?

- A) 25
- B) 50 ✓
- C) 75
- D) 100

**Q38.** A student scores 72 out of 80. What is the percentage?

- A) 72%
- B) 80%
- C) 85%
- D) 90% ✓

#### Profit and Loss Questions

**Q40.** A shopkeeper buys a pen for ₹10 and sells it for ₹12. What is the profit percentage?

- A) 10%
- B) 15%
- C) 20% ✓
- D) 25%

**Answer:** C) 20%
**Explanation:** Profit = 12 - 10 = ₹2. Profit% = (2/10) × 100 = 20%.

**Q41.** If cost price = ₹500 and selling price = ₹450, find the loss percentage.

- A) 5%
- B) 10% ✓
- C) 15%
- D) 20%

**Answer:** B) 10%
**Explanation:** Loss = 500 - 450 = ₹50. Loss% = (50/500) × 100 = 10%.

**Q42.** A man buys an article for ₹800 and sells it at a profit of 15%. Find the selling price.

- A) ₹900
- B) ₹920 ✓
- C) ₹940
- D) ₹960

**Answer:** B) ₹920
**Explanation:** Profit = 15% of 800 = ₹120. Selling price = 800 + 120 = ₹920.

#### LCM and HCF Questions

**Q43.** Find the HCF of 24 and 36.

- A) 6
- B) 12 ✓
- C) 18
- D) 24

**Answer:** B) 12
**Explanation:** Factors of 24: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24. Factors of 36: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 18, 36. HCF = 12.

**Q44.** Find the LCM of 8, 12, and 16.

- A) 24
- B) 32
- C) 48 ✓
- D) 96

**Answer:** C) 48
**Explanation:** Multiples of 8: 8, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48... Multiples of 12: 12, 24, 36, 48... Multiples of 16: 16, 32, 48... LCM = 48.

**Q45.** The HCF of two numbers is 8 and their LCM is 96. If one number is 24, find the other.

- A) 32 ✓
- B) 36
- C) 48
- D) 72

**Answer:** A) 32
**Explanation:** Product of two numbers = HCF × LCM. 24 × x = 8 × 96. x = 768/24 = 32.

#### Average Questions

**Q46.** Find the average of 10, 20, 30, 40, 50.

- A) 25
- B) 30 ✓
- C) 35
- D) 40

**Answer:** B) 30
**Explanation:** Sum = 10 + 20 + 30 + 40 + 50 = 150. Average = 150/5 = 30.

**Q47.** The average of 5 numbers is 18. If one number is removed, the average becomes 16. What is the removed number?

- A) 20
- B) 24
- C) 26 ✓
- D) 28

**Answer:** C) 26
**Explanation:** Total of 5 numbers = 5 × 18 = 90. Total of 4 numbers = 4 × 16 = 64. Removed number = 90 - 64 = 26.

**Q48.** The average weight of 3 students is 40 kg. A new student joins and the average becomes 42 kg. What is the weight of the new student?

- A) 46 kg
- B) 48 kg ✓
- C) 50 kg
- D) 52 kg

**Answer:** B) 48 kg
**Explanation:** Total weight of 3 = 3 × 40 = 120 kg. Total weight of 4 = 4 × 42 = 168 kg. New student's weight = 168 - 120 = 48 kg.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Here are the most common questions about JNVST Class 6 preparation:

### Q1. What is the JNVST exam?

**A:** JNVST stands for Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test. It is a national-level entrance exam conducted by Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (NVS) for admission to Class 6 in Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas across India.

### Q2. Who is eligible for JNVST Class 6?

**A:** Students studying in Class 5 in a recognized school in the same district where the JNV is located are eligible. Age should be between 9-13 years.

### Q3. What is the exam pattern for JNVST 2027?

**A:** The exam has 80 MCQs across three sections: Mental Ability (40 questions), Arithmetic (20 questions), and Language (20 questions). Total duration is 2 hours with no negative marking.

### Q4. How can I prepare for JNVST in 3 months?

**A:** Follow a structured plan: Month 1 for concept building, Month 2 for practice, Month 3 for mock tests and revision. Practice at least 2-3 hours daily with focus on weak areas.

### Q5. What are the most important topics for JNVST Mental Ability?

**A:** Figure Series, Mirror Image, Coding-Decoding, Analogy, Number Series, and Odd One Out are the most important topics, carrying 60-70% of the Mental Ability section.

### Q6. Is there negative marking in JNVST?

**A:** No, there is no negative marking in JNVST. Students should attempt all questions.

### Q7. What is the minimum qualifying marks for JNVST?

**A:** The qualifying marks vary by category. General category students typically need 50-60%, while reserved categories have lower cut-offs.

### Q8. How many times can I appear for JNVST?

**A:** A student can appear for JNVST only once for Class 6 admission.

### Q9. What is the medium of the JNVST exam?

**A:** The exam is conducted in English, Hindi, and the regional language of the state.

### Q10. How many seats are available in JNVs?

**A:** There are approximately 661 Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas across India with about 80 seats per school for Class 6 admission.

### Q11. Can I use a calculator in JNVST?

**A:** No, calculators and electronic devices are not allowed in the JNVST exam.

### Q12. What is the best time to start JNVST preparation?

**A:** Ideally, start preparation 6-12 months before the exam. However, focused preparation for 3-4 months can also yield good results.

### Q13. Are JNVST questions repeated from previous years?

**A:** While exact questions are rarely repeated, similar patterns and question types appear regularly. Practising previous year papers helps understand these patterns.

### Q14. How important is the Mental Ability section?

**A:** Very important. Mental Ability carries 50% of the total marks (40 out of 80). Strong performance in this section significantly boosts overall score.

### Q15. What is the difference between JNVST and other entrance exams?

**A:** JNVST is specifically for Navodaya Vidyalaya admission and focuses on mental ability, basic arithmetic, and language skills. It is designed for rural and semi-urban students.
