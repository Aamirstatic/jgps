## What is Sainik School?

Sainik Schools are residential schools run by the Sainik School Society under the Ministry of Defence, Government of India. There are 33 Sainik Schools across India. The main goal is to prepare students for entry into the National Defence Academy (NDA) and Indian Armed Forces.

These schools offer quality education along with military-style discipline, physical training, and leadership development. Students live on campus, follow a strict routine, and grow into confident, disciplined young adults.

In 2021, the government also started "New Sainik Schools" — these are existing private schools that follow the Sainik School model. Currently, there are 35+ New Sainik Schools across India.

### Why Sainik School?

- **Defence career foundation** — Best preparation for NDA, Navy, Air Force
- **Discipline and character** — Military-style routine builds strong habits
- **Quality education** — CBSE curriculum with experienced teachers
- **All-round development** — Sports, NCC, adventure activities
- **Affordable fees** — Government subsidized, much cheaper than private boarding schools
- **Alumni network** — Many Sainik School alumni are senior military officers, IAS officers, and leaders

## Eligibility Criteria for AISSEE 2027

Before filling the form, check if your child is eligible. NTA is strict about eligibility — wrong details mean rejection.

### Class 6 Eligibility

| Criteria | Details |
|---|---|
| **Nationality** | Indian citizens only |
| **Gender** | Boys and Girls both eligible |
| **Age Limit** | 10 to 12 years as on 31 March 2027 |
| **Date of Birth** | 1 April 2015 to 31 March 2017 (both dates inclusive) |

## AISSEE 2027 Exam Pattern

Understanding the exam pattern is the first step to preparation. The exam is conducted offline (pen and paper) in OMR sheet format.

### Class 6 Exam Pattern

| Subject | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mathematics | 50 | 150 | — |
| General Knowledge (Science & Social Studies) | 25 | 50 | — |
| Language | 25 | 50 | — |
| Intelligence/Reasoning | 25 | 50 | — |
| **Total** | **125** | **300** | **2 hours 30 minutes** |

- Each correct answer: +2.4 marks (Mathematics), +2 marks (other sections)
- **No negative marking**
- Exam medium: English, Hindi, or Local Language of the State

### Class 9 Exam Pattern

| Subject | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mathematics | 50 | 200 | — |
| English | 25 | 50 | — |
| Intelligence/Reasoning | 25 | 50 | — |
| General Science | 25 | 50 | — |
| Social Studies | 25 | 50 | — |
| **Total** | **150** | **400** | **3 hours** |

- Each correct answer: +4 marks (Mathematics), +2 marks (other sections)
- **No negative marking**
- Exam medium: **English only** for Class 9

## How to Fill AISSEE 2027 Application Form

The application process is completely online. Follow these steps:

**Step 1: Registration**
- Visit [https://exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society/](https://exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society/)
- Click "Apply for AISSEE 2027"
- Enter basic details: Name, Date of Birth, Email, Mobile Number
- Create login credentials

**Step 2: Fill Application Form**
- Login with your credentials
- Enter personal details, address, parent information
- Select Class (6 or 9) and preferred Sainik School
- Enter educational qualifications

**Step 3: Upload Documents**
- Recent passport-size photograph (JPEG, 10-200 KB)
- Signature (JPEG, 4-30 KB)
- Birth certificate
- Class 5/8 marksheet
- Category certificate (if applicable)

**Step 4: Pay Fee**
- General/OBC/Defence: Rs. 850/-
- SC/ST: Rs. 700/-
- Payment mode: Online (Debit Card/Credit Card/Net Banking/UPI)

**Step 5: Submit and Print**
- Review all details carefully
- Submit the form
- Download and print the confirmation page

**Tip:** Keep all documents ready before starting. The form cannot be saved as draft — you must complete it in one session.

## 5 Mistakes Parents Must Avoid

**Mistake 1: Waiting for Notification to Start Preparation**
The notification comes in September/October. The exam is in January. That gives you only 3 months. Smart parents start in June-July. By the time the form comes, your child should be halfway through preparation.

**Mistake 2: Ignoring the Age Limit**
Many parents fill the form without checking the exact date of birth range. If your child's birthday falls outside the range (even by one day), the form will be rejected. Check the date of birth carefully: Class 6 — 1 April 2015 to 31 March 2017.

**Mistake 3: Using Old Syllabus Books**
The AISSEE pattern has changed over the years. Old books may have outdated question patterns. Always use 2027 edition books specifically designed for AISSEE. NCERT books for Class 5 and 8 are the foundation — use them first.

**Mistake 4: No Mock Test Practice**
Your child may know the answers. But can they solve 125 questions in 2.5 hours under exam pressure? Without mock tests, they will run out of time. Start weekly mock tests at least 8 weeks before the exam.

**Mistake 5: Ignoring Physical Fitness**
Sainik School is not just about academics. After clearing the written exam, there is a medical test and physical fitness assessment. Children who are overweight or have health issues may get rejected. Start physical activity — running, exercise, sports — from Day 1.

## Preparation Tips for AISSEE 2027

### 4-Month Study Plan

**Month 1 (September 2026) — Build Basics**
- Study NCERT Class 5/8 textbooks thoroughly
- Focus on Mathematics fundamentals
- Start reading a newspaper daily (for GK and language)
- Practice 20 reasoning questions daily

**Month 2 (October 2026) — Strengthen Weak Areas**
- Identify weak subjects through a diagnostic test
- Practice previous year AISSEE papers
- Start learning new vocabulary words (5 words daily)
- Increase math practice to 30 questions daily

**Month 3 (November 2026) — Practice and Speed**
- Solve one full mock test every Sunday
- Focus on time management
- Revise GK topics: History, Geography, Science
- Practice comprehension passages daily

**Month 4 (December 2026) — Final Revision**
- Take mock tests every alternate day
- Revise all formulas and important facts
- Focus on accuracy, not speed
- Light study in the last week — rest is important

## People Also Ask

**Q: When will Sainik School Admission 2027 form come?**
A: The AISSEE 2027 application form is expected to be released in September or October 2026. Last year, the form was available from 10 October 2025. Check the official NTA website regularly for updates.

**Q: Can girls apply for Sainik School admission 2027?**
A: Yes. Both boys and girls can apply for Class 6 admission in all Sainik Schools. For Class 9, girls can apply subject to vacancy. Since 2021, girls have been allowed in Sainik Schools.

**Q: What is the age limit for Sainik School Class 6 in 2027?**
A: The student should be between 10 to 12 years old as on 31 March 2027. This means the date of birth should be between 1 April 2015 and 31 March 2017.

**Q: Is there negative marking in AISSEE 2027?**
A: No. There is no negative marking in AISSEE 2027. Students should attempt all questions even if they are unsure of the answer.

**Q: How many Sainik Schools are there in India?**
A: There are 33 Sainik Schools run by the Sainik School Society, plus 35+ New Sainik Schools across India. The government plans to add more in the coming years.

**Q: What is the fee of Sainik School per year?**
A: The annual fee for Sainik Schools ranges from approximately Rs. 85,000 to Rs. 1,35,000 including tuition, hostel, mess, uniform, and other charges. SC/ST students get fee concessions.

**Q: Is coaching necessary for AISSEE preparation?**
A: Coaching can help but is not mandatory. Many students crack AISSEE through self-study using NCERT books, previous year papers, and online resources. The key is consistent daily practice of 2-3 hours.
